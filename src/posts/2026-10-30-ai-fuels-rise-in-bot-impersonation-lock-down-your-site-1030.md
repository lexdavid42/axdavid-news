**TL;DR:** Google engineer Gary Illyes says AI has made bot impersonation worse, with rogue bots increasingly pretending to be trusted crawlers.

**Why it matters:** Fake crawlers can scrape content, skew analytics, abuse APIs, and trick systems that treat certain bots as safe. Freelancers and small businesses who rely on search, clean metrics, or API access can lose traffic, revenue, or data integrity.

**What to do:**
- Verify important crawlers using reverse DNS and published IP ranges before granting elevated access.
- Add rate limits, bot-detection rules, and authentication to sensitive endpoints.
- Monitor unusual crawl patterns and block repeat impersonators with WAF rules.
