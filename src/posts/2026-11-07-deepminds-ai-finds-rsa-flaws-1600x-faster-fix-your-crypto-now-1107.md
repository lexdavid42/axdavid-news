**TL;DR:** Google DeepMind used AI agents to surface weaknesses in legacy RSA cryptography up to 1,600x faster than before.

**Why it matters:** This speeds up how quickly attackers — and defenders — can discover weak keys and flawed implementations. Small businesses and freelancers who rely on hosted sites, payment processors, or legacy integrations could be exposed without warning. A quick audit can prevent downtime, breaches, or lost client trust.

**What to do:**
- Check with your host or IT provider that your site and services use modern TLS and up-to-date crypto libraries.
- Rotate private keys and certificates now if they haven’t been changed in years.
- Run a security scan or hire a short-term audit if you handle payments or sensitive client data.
