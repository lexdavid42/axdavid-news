**TL;DR:** Ziff Davis says Google referrals plunged about 37% as AI-driven search overviews and summary features siphon clicks away from publisher sites.

**Why it matters:** If you depend on Google for visitors, expect sudden drops in traffic and ad revenue. Publishers are already cutting costs and writing down assets. Freelancers and small publishers must treat search referrals as volatile and plan for lower organic income.

**What to do:**
- Audit how much of your traffic and revenue comes from Google and model a 30–40% loss.
- Build direct channels: email lists, memberships, social funnels, and paid products to own revenue.
- Optimize content for AI snippets: add concise answers, structured data, and premium gated analysis when possible.
