**TL;DR:** Google’s updated privacy policy says content you share with AI-powered Search from Gmail, Drive or Photos may be used to personalize ads.

**Why it matters:** This can mix business or client content into Google’s ad targeting and model signals. Entrepreneurs who run client work, proposals, or private drafts through Google’s AI features could inadvertently expose sensitive info or change how results are personalized.

**What to do:**
- Audit what you feed into Google’s AI Search and stop sending customer PII or confidential work.
- Review your Google account privacy and Ads personalization settings; consider pausing AI Search features for sensitive accounts.
- Optional: Move sensitive prompts to paid or self-hosted AI tools with explicit data-use guarantees.
