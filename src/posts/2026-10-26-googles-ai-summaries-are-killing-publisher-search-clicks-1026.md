**TL;DR:** Google’s AI-powered search now delivers answers and summaries inside search results, letting users get the story without clicking through — and publishers are losing referral traffic.

**Why it matters:** If you depend on search for readers or clients, expect fewer visits and lower ad or affiliate revenue. AI summaries turn your article into a one-paragraph answer that stays on Google. That shifts value away from publishers to the search layer.

**What to do:**
- Audit your highest-traffic pages and add clear conversion hooks (email sign-up, lead magnet, product CTA) above the fold.
- Use concise, structured leads and schema (FAQ, article) so Google credits and links your site when it creates summaries.
- Diversify: prioritize newsletters, social channels, and repeat-audience products (paid tiers, memberships).
