**TL;DR:** Google released Gemini 4 Carbon, a new Gemini-family model focused on cybersecurity, arriving shortly after the Argon release.

**Why it matters:** This gives entrepreneurs and freelancers a purpose-built AI that can help spot vulnerabilities, speed incident triage, and automate security checks. Faster, AI-assisted security work cuts billable hours and lowers breach risk for small teams.

**What to do:**
- Sign up or request access and test the model on non‑production code and configs.
- Use it to automate routine scans and flag probable issues, but verify every result manually.
- Consider adding security audits or AI-powered hardening to your services to win higher-fee clients.
