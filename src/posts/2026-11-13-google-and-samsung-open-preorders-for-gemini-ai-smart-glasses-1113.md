**TL;DR:** Google and Samsung opened preorders for Gemini-powered smart glasses from Warby Parker and Gentle Monster, starting at $395, with sales slated to begin December 10.

**Why it matters:** A new consumer-class headset brings hands-free AI into reach for freelancers and small-business owners. It can reduce friction for quick lookups, translations, or note-taking when you can’t stop working to grab a phone. Early pricing and preorder windows let you plan purchasing and testing before widespread adoption.

**What to do:**
- Decide if hands-free AI would save you time; set aside $395+ for a preorder.
- Reserve a frame or demo slot now so you can test fit, comfort, and real-world usefulness before Dec. 10.
- Follow early reviews and privacy coverage; delay buying if your work demands strict confidentiality.
