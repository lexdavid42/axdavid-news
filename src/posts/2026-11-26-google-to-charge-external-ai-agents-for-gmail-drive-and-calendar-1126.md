**TL;DR:** Google will start charging external AI agents that heavily access Gmail, Calendar and Drive on April 1, 2027.

**Why it matters:** This changes the cost math for anyone building or relying on third‑party assistants that read or write your Google data. Expect new per‑use charges, API limits, and billing thresholds that can quickly raise operating costs. Freelancers who use agent services or developers selling agent integrations must reprice and redesign to avoid surprise bills.

**What to do:**
- Audit every AI agent you use or build to map how many Gmail, Drive or Calendar calls they make.
- Add usage caps, batching, and caching to reduce hot‑path calls and estimate monthly fees.
- (Optional) Offer a paid plan or ask clients to connect their own Google accounts to shift billing.
