**TL;DR:** Google has rolled out Gemini 4 Argon globally to developers and enterprises via the Gemini App, AI Mode, and the Gemini API.

**Why it matters:** This gives freelancers and small teams access to a top-tier model for multi-step tasks and complex workflows. You can prototype powerful features without waiting for limited beta access. Wider availability shortens development time and raises the ceiling for product capabilities.

**What to do:**
- Sign up for the Gemini API and test Argon on a small prototype feature.
- Prioritize use cases that need deep reasoning or multi-step automation, not simple prompts.
- Review Google’s access terms and pricing before scaling.
