**TL;DR:** Adobe is expanding Acrobat into Gmail and Google Drive, adding Gemini connectors and PDF editing inside Google Workspace so Acrobat can run AI tasks in the background.

**Why it matters:** You spend hours extracting, editing, and routing PDFs in client work. In-place PDF edits, smart summaries, and AI extraction inside email and Drive cut those chores. That means faster contract turnarounds, quicker invoice processing, and fewer manual copy‑pastes.

**What to do:**
- Open a recent contract in Gmail or Drive and use Acrobat’s AI to extract key dates, payment terms, and parties.
- Add Gemini connectors to your proposal and invoice templates to auto-populate fields from emails and Drive files.
- Optional: Pilot accepting minor edits in-place (with version history) to speed approvals and reduce back‑and‑forth.
