**TL;DR:** OpenAI and Google are expanding advertising inside their conversational AI interfaces, betting that chat queries reveal stronger purchase intent than traditional search.

**Why it matters:** AI chat interfaces are becoming a new place to reach buyers who describe needs conversationally. That means higher‑intent leads and fewer clicks to convert. Freelancers and small businesses can get early advantage by adapting offers to short, chat‑friendly funnels.

**What to do:**
- Audit your top offers and rewrite the pitch as a 1–2 line chat response that answers a buyer’s immediate need.
- Run quick experiments: adapt one landing page for zero‑click answers and one for a fast checkout flow.
- Track conversions and privacy policies for each AI platform and adjust messaging accordingly.
