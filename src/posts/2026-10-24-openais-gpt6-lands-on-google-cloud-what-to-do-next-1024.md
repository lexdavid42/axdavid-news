**TL;DR:** OpenAI’s top models, including GPT‑6, are now available to run on Google Cloud and will operate under Google Cloud’s enterprise security and governance controls.

**Why it matters:** You can run OpenAI models where your business already stores data and manages access. That reduces integration friction and gives enterprises familiar compliance and governance tools. Freelancers and small teams can test the newest models without switching clouds.

**What to do:**
- Talk to your Google Cloud rep or account contact to confirm availability, access methods, and pricing for OpenAI models.
- Build a quick proof of concept calling a GPT‑6 endpoint from a test app to measure latency, cost, and reliability.
- If you handle client data, ask specifically about data residency, logging, and governance controls before moving to production.
