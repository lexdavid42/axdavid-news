**TL;DR:** Google’s Gemini app now lets U.S. users connect Google Wallet via Plaid so the AI can analyze spending and flag forgotten subscriptions.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and small-business owners can get a fast, AI-powered snapshot of cash flow and recurring costs. That saves time you’d otherwise spend hunting receipts or digging through bank statements. It’s useful for budgeting and spotting wasted subscriptions — but it involves sensitive financial data, so be cautious.

**What to do:**
- Connect Google Wallet to Gemini through Plaid (U.S. users) and give the app read access only if you’re comfortable.
- Ask Gemini for a spending summary and a list of recurring charges, then copy the breakdown into your bookkeeping or budget.
- Optional: review and revoke Plaid permissions, enable 2FA, and keep a local record of any changes.
