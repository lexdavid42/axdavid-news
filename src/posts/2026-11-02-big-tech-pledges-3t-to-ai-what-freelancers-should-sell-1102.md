**TL;DR:** Big Tech has committed over $3 trillion to AI, with Google topping $1 trillion and Meta over $800 billion.

**Why it matters:** That level of spending will create a flood of paid AI projects and new vendor demand. It also raises compute and hiring costs, squeezing small shops that don't price for AI overhead. Expect more RFPs for integrations, tooling and migration work.

**What to do:**
- Add a clear AI audit offering: 1–2 week assessments that map ROI and compute needs.
- Price compute and model usage separately and include a cost-estimate line in every proposal.
- Learn one fast deployment pattern (chatbot, fine-tune, or prompt workflow) and productize it as a repeatable sprint.
