**TL;DR:** Adobe previewed integrations that let trusted third‑party AI agents make edits inside Photoshop and Premiere.

**Why it matters:** This hands AI-powered helpers to creative pros and freelancers, letting them automate repetitive edits and speed delivery. It also creates new income streams for plugin makers and editor-for-hire services that manage agent workflows.

**What to do:**
- Check Adobe’s preview or beta sign-up and test a small task to see how agent edits fit your workflow.
- Review licensing, ownership, and export settings before using agent edits on client work.
- Optional: Scout or list agent services that can run routine edits you’d rather outsource.
