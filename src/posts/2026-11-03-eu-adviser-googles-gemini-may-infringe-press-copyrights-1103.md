**TL;DR:** An adviser to Europe’s top court says Google’s Gemini may, in some cases, infringe press copyrights when it uses news content to answer queries.

**Why it matters:** This could change what AI chatbots can legally reuse from publishers. If courts follow the adviser’s view, products that summarize or republish news could face limits or new licensing costs. Freelancers and small apps that rely on free news scraping or unlicensed summaries may lose access or face legal risk.

**What to do:**
- Stop republishing press content verbatim from AI outputs and add independent value to summaries.
- Switch to licensed news feeds or negotiate permissions before building news-based features.
- Monitor EU rulings and adjust Europe-facing products and contracts.
