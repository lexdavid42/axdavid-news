**TL;DR:** Google’s Gemini now lets people share AI-generated avatars with friends, tag others in prompts, and create group images and videos featuring multiple likenesses.

**Why it matters:** This turns AI avatars from solo novelty into a social content tool. Freelancers and creators can offer collaborative image or campaign services, but they must manage consent and rights when using others’ likenesses. Expect new client requests for group-based promotional visuals.

**What to do:**
- Update service offerings: add group-avatar shoots and multi-person AI visuals to your portfolio.
- Create a simple consent checklist and written release for using clients’ likenesses in AI outputs.
- Test Gemini’s sharing flow now to learn limits, tag features, and export quality.
