**TL;DR:** Adobe has integrated its CX Enterprise Coworker with major AI platforms (OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, Anthropic, Databricks and others), letting businesses run Adobe’s customer‑experience assistant on the model of their choice.

**Why it matters:** This lowers vendor lock‑in for customer support and marketing automation. Freelancers and small agencies can pick the cheapest or most accurate model for each client without rebuilding the CX layer. It creates new chances to sell multi‑model implementation and optimization services.

**What to do:**
- Run a quick pilot: test the Coworker on two different LLM backends and compare response quality and cost.
- Pitch a model‑selection audit to clients who use automated support or marketing workflows.
- Build template bundles optimized for multiple model backends and sell them as migration‑ready packages.
