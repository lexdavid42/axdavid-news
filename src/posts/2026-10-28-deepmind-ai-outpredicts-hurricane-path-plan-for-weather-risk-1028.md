**TL;DR:** Google DeepMind’s AI model beat traditional forecasts on Hurricane Isaias’ track, correctly pointing it to Florida’s western Panhandle while older models leaned farther west.

**Why it matters:** Better path forecasts cut false alarms and improve timing for closures, staffing, and logistics. The National Hurricane Center now blends AI and physics models, with humans still guiding hazard messaging. The AI was stronger on track but weaker at estimating storm intensity.

**What to do:**
- Audit which parts of your operations depend on weather-driven timing (deliveries, staffing, events).
- Ask your data or logistics providers whether they can include AI-based track forecasts or blended NHC products.
- Optional: run a short test using an alternative forecast feed for one region before changing standard procedures.
