**TL;DR:** Google’s AI Mode may get a Library for saved outputs, plus pinned chats and the ability to rename threads to make generated content easier to find.

**Why it matters:** These additions turn transient AI chats into reusable assets. Freelancers and founders who reuse prompts, templates, or marketing copy will save time and reduce duplicated work. Better chat organization speeds handoffs and client revisions.

**What to do:**
- Start noting which chats and prompts you reuse so you can pin or move them into the Library when it arrives.
- Create a short naming convention for threads now (client, project, date) so renamed threads stay consistent.
- Optional: Try current AI Mode beta features (image uploads, follow-ups) to build workflows you can drop into the new Library.
