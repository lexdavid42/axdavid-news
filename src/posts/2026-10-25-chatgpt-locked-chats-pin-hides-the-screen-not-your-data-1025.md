**TL;DR:** ChatGPT’s new Locked Chats adds a PIN that blocks casual viewers on your device, but it does not change how your conversations are stored or handled.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and small-business owners often paste client names, passwords, and strategy into chats. A PIN stops someone at your keyboard, but it won’t replace proper data controls or legal agreements. Treat this as screen-level protection only.

**What to do:**
- Don’t paste client PII, credentials, or confidential contracts into ChatGPT unless you’ve confirmed the service’s data-use terms.
- Move sensitive work to end-to-end encrypted tools, self‑hosted models, or vendors that guarantee no-data use for training.
- Keep device security and written client consent in place when you use third-party AI.
