**TL;DR:** CrowdStrike warns AI-driven cybercrime surged 89%, with automated scams outpacing old-school malware and a victim losing life savings to a fake airline app.

**Why it matters:** Scammers are using AI to scale convincing, automated attacks. Solo founders, freelancers, and small agencies are prime targets because they handle payments, client data, and remote onboarding.

**What to do:**
- Require two-factor authentication and use payment platforms with chargeback protection.
- Verify new clients and apps through a short live video or trusted identity checks before sharing funds or sensitive access.
- Run basic site and dependency scans monthly and patch PHP or plugin vulnerabilities promptly.
