**TL;DR:** Google Play is introducing AI creator tools: vibe-coded app templates, Ask Play recommendation help, and Gamer DNA profiles to personalize games.

**Why it matters:** These tools let small teams and solo creators spin up functional apps without heavy engineering. Faster prototyping cuts time to first sale and lowers development costs. That amplifies limited marketing budgets quickly.

**What to do:**
- Use the vibe-coded templates to build a landing-page MVP, price setup or customization work, and offer white-label builds.
- Query Ask Play to generate niche app concepts, onboarding flows, and app store copy you can sell or test quickly.
- Optional: Create modular game templates tuned to Gamer DNA profiles and license variants or run paid A/B experiments.
