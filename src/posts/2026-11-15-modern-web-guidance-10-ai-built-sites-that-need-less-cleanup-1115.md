**TL;DR:** Google released Modern Web Guidance 1.0 — a set of updated standards and expert rules for AI coding agents to build modern, production-ready websites.

**Why it matters:** AI tools will now have a clearer checklist to follow, so sites they generate should need less manual rework. Freelancers and agencies can deliver faster and reduce revision rounds. This lowers billable hours spent fixing basic layout, accessibility, and performance issues.

**What to do:**
- Update your AI coding prompts to require compliance with "Modern Web Guidance 1.0" and call out accessibility and performance checks.
- Add a short pre-delivery QA pass: responsiveness, accessibility basics, and core web vitals.
- Optional: bake example pages that match the guidance into your prompt library or automation.
