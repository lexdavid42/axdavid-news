**TL;DR:** Google’s Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is now generally available — it produces and edits up to 15‑second AI videos via chat and is about 30% cheaper (roughly $0.07/sec at 720p).

**Why it matters:** This makes short promotional video production faster and far cheaper. Chat-based editing reduces the need for complex video tools or long edit cycles. Draft in low resolution and upscale the final cut to save money without losing quality.

**What to do:**
- Test a 15‑second product or social ad using the Gemini Omni API to compare speed and cost with your current workflow.
- Draft in low resolution, iterate by chat, then upscale only the final clip to cut costs.
- If you make videos for clients, add a fixed-rate micro‑video package (15s) to your services and automate generation via the API.
