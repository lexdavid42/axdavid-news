**TL;DR:** Google says its News AI pilot now includes more than 400 partner publications, covering publishers of all sizes.

**Why it matters:** This shifts how search and AI tools will surface reporting and summaries. If your content gets included, it can show up inside AI features that users consult first. That creates potential new referral and licensing channels for freelancers, local outlets, and niche newsletters.

**What to do:**
- Check Google’s publisher programs and announcements to see if your outlet or freelance work is eligible.
- Make articles scannable for AI: clear ledes, concise summaries, and straightforward attribution.
- Monitor traffic sources and add analytics tags so you can spot AI-driven referrals quickly.
