**TL;DR:** Adobe expanded its partnership with Google to embed Google's advanced AI models across Adobe apps — including Photoshop, Acrobat, Premiere and Adobe Express — and to bring Acrobat editing directly into Gmail and Drive.

**Why it matters:** That puts powerful generative and conversational AI where freelancers already work. You can cut manual PDF and image edits, speed client revisions, and keep more tasks inside Google Workspace. Faster output means more billable hours or the ability to take on extra clients.

**What to do:**
- Test in Drive/Gmail as Acrobat editing rolls out; use conversational edits to fix, summarize, or reformat PDFs.
- Try Photoshop’s AI-powered edits for rapid mockups and client revisions; preserve originals and compare results.
- Optional: follow Adobe/Google beta notes and update your service list and pricing to sell faster AI-enabled deliverables.
