**TL;DR:** A study of 88,000 Google reviews found AI use rose much faster in business owner replies than in customer reviews.

**Why it matters:** That gap means businesses are already comfortable using AI to manage reputation and respond fast. Freelancers and small agencies can sell AI-powered review-reply services that save clients time and protect ratings. Keep humans in the loop so tone and facts stay correct.

**What to do:**
- Launch an AI-assisted review-reply offering with clear SLAs (fast turnaround, tone options).
- Create a short prompt kit and checklist to ensure replies stay accurate and compliant.
- Optional: Package monthly monitoring plus escalation for complex or legal complaints.
