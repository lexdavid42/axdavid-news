**TL;DR:** Google has launched AI-powered shopping in Canada, letting customers buy from select retailers through chat with its Gemini agent and a single checkout cart.

**Why it matters:** This opens a new direct sales channel where customers complete purchases inside AI conversations. Merchants on partners like Shopify and select retailers can capture conversions without sending buyers to separate checkout pages. Expect broader retailer access in 2027.

**What to do:**
- If you sell on Shopify or work with supported retailers, check for Google’s integration and opt in when available.
- Prepare product metadata, shipping and return policies so the AI can answer questions and complete orders.
- Optional: run small tests on product sets to measure conversion lift and optimize chat prompts.
