**TL;DR:** Meta has paused proposed web‑scraping contract terms after publisher pushback, signaling a retreat from aggressive site‑crawling policies.

**Why it matters:** Publishers pushed back on terms that would let AI firms crawl business‑linked pages. That could limit free access to raw web data freelancers and small firms use for research, curation, and model training. Expect tougher contracts, more paid data access, and new scraping risk.

**What to do:**
- Audit where your tools pull data from and switch to licensed APIs where possible.
- Budget for paid data or broker agreements and document data provenance for clients.
- Optional: build partnerships with publishers or use synthetic/consented datasets instead.
