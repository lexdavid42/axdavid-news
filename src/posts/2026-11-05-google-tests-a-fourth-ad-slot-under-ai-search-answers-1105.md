**TL;DR:** Google has started showing a fourth paid ad under some AI-generated search answers in AI Mode, stacking multiple ads directly beneath the AI overview.

**Why it matters:** That extra ad slot can push organic results further down and compress click-throughs for small sites and freelancers. Paid placements now sit closer to the concise AI answer users read first, changing where attention — and conversions — land.

**What to do:**
- Monitor your Google Search Console and analytics for sudden drops in organic CTR on queries you rank for.
- Test small, targeted PPC bids on high-value queries where AI Mode shows answers; measure cost per lead closely.
- Optimize concise, AI-friendly content (short bullets, clear answers) to win the overview or the follow-up clicks.
