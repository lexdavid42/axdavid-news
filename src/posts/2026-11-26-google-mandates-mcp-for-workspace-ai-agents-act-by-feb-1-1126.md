**TL;DR:** Google will require AI agents to use the Model Context Protocol (MCP) to access Workspace data starting Feb 1, 2027.

**Why it matters:** This affects any agent that reads or writes Workspace data on your behalf. Noncompliant tools could lose access or face new permission hurdles. Small teams and freelancers who rely on automated inbox, calendar or document workflows should expect integration work.

**What to do:**
- Inventory every AI agent, plugin, or integration that touches your Workspace data. Identify vendor-built and in-house agents.
- Ask vendors or update your code to support MCP well before Feb 1, 2027; treat this as a required integration step.
- Test agents in a sandbox Workspace, review granted scopes, and revoke access for tools that won't comply.
