**TL;DR:** Criminals are stealing AI API keys to run costly compute and access paid AI services without permission.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and small teams often rely on API keys to power client work and automation. Stolen keys can interrupt workflows and create unexpectedly large bills. Securing keys stops attacks before they hit your bank account or reputation.

**What to do:**
- Rotate and revoke keys now; issue short‑lived tokens where possible.
- Restrict keys by IP, service scope and billing project; enable spending alerts.
- Store secrets in a manager, audit usage logs regularly and require MFA for accounts.
