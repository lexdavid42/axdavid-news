**TL;DR:** A leak claims Google DeepMind’s upcoming Gemini 4 “Xenon” outperforms Anthropic’s Fable 5.5 while offering much lower inference costs.

**Why it matters:** If true, cheaper, higher‑quality models cut your AI bill and let you add features or lower prices. Tool and API choices could shift fast, changing where you host inference and which partners you pick. This matters for margins, pricing, and which workflows you automate.

**What to do:**
- Watch provider pricing and performance announcements this week and next.
- Run small A/B tests on critical prompts to see cost vs. quality tradeoffs.
- Refactor heavy inference paths (batching, caching, smaller models) so you can switch providers without a rewrite.
