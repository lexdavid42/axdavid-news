**TL;DR:** Google’s experiment to pay publishers for content that shapes its AI answers could shift value from clicks to the content that trains and fuels AI responses.

**Why it matters:** This changes how content earns revenue. Instead of chasing ranking tweaks, entrepreneurs and freelancers must aim to be the source AI cites. That creates new monetization paths for the right content.

**What to do:**
- Audit your best-performing pages and turn each into a short, answer-first resource focused on one clear query.
- Add explicit sourcing signals: clear author info, data points, and short summaries that make content easy for AI to extract.
- Track referral and engagement changes weekly to spot pages that start feeding AI answers and treat them as premium assets.
