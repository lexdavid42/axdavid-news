**TL;DR:** Meta says its new AI ad placements return about 5% more conversions and drive roughly 9% higher spend, and it claims some budgets could move away from Google Search.

**Why it matters:** Client ad dollars are limited. A measurable shift from search to Meta could change where you get clicks and conversions. Test quickly or you risk losing share—or wasting money chasing an unverified trend.

**What to do:**
- Run a small, controlled A/B test moving 5–10% of a top-performing campaign to Meta AI Ads.
- Measure conversions, CPA, and post-click revenue for 2–3 weeks and compare to search.
- Optional: If performance holds, reallocate incrementally and keep strict attribution rules.
