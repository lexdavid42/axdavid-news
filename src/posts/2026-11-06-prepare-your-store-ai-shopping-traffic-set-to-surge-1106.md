**TL;DR:** Adobe forecasts AI-driven shopping traffic to U.S. retail sites will jump 130% this holiday season while Google rolls out a Universal Cart to connect buyers across stores.

**Why it matters:** AI shopping will change where customers start and finish purchases. That shifts traffic away from traditional product pages and into AI-driven summaries and carts. Merchants who don't expose clean product data risk losing conversions.

**What to do:**
- Audit and fix your product feeds: titles, images, SKUs, prices and schema are priority.
- Prepare checkout APIs and test a Universal Cart flow or equivalent to keep control of the sale.
- Monitor AI-shopping metrics and update ad/SEO spend toward prompts and product queries.
