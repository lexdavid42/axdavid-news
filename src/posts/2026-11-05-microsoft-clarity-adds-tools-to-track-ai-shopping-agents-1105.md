**TL;DR:** Microsoft updated Clarity with three new features to help merchants see how people and AI shopping agents interact with their sites.

**Why it matters:** AI shopping agents are starting to browse and buy for customers. That can change conversions, cart behavior, and return rates. Visibility into agent actions helps you fix problems before they cost sales.

**What to do:**
- Install or update Microsoft Clarity on your store and enable the new features.
- Monitor sessions flagged as agent-driven to spot navigation or checkout friction.
- Simplify product metadata and checkout steps so agents can parse and complete purchases.
