**TL;DR:** Google’s Search AI Mode can now optionally read your Google Wallet; Drive and the Gemini app are scheduled to be added in the coming weeks.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and small‑business owners can get instant, account-aware answers — think card reward tips, spending summaries, and travel-perk advice — without digging through statements. That can shave hours from finance and admin work. It also requires careful permission checks: you’re choosing what personal data the AI can access.

**What to do:**
- Opt into Wallet’s read-only access in AI Mode and ask for quick spending and rewards summaries to speed bookkeeping.
- When Drive integration appears, use it to have AI summarize invoices, contracts, and project folders, but keep sensitive PII out of files you don’t want scanned.
- Regularly review AI Mode permissions and revoke access if results feel off or expose too much data.
