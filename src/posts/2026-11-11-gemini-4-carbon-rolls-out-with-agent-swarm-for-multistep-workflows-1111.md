**TL;DR:** Google rolled out Gemini 4 Carbon worldwide and added an agent “swarm” feature that coordinates multiple AI agents to tackle multi-step tasks.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and small teams can offload complex workflows to coordinated agents instead of juggling prompts. That can cut time on research, drafting, outreach and repetitive client work. It creates a simple path to productize automation services.

**What to do:**
- Sign up or upgrade access and run a simple pilot: use the swarm to automate one repeatable client task.
- Map the steps you want automated, then build and test a 2–3 agent flow before scaling.
- Price an “agent-run” package and offer a trial to an existing client.
