**TL;DR:** Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers can now claim and manage monthly Google Cloud credits directly inside Google AI Studio.

**Why it matters:** This removes a billing step that often slows down experiments and prototypes. Entrepreneurs and freelancers can run model tests and short-term workloads without juggling separate cloud invoices. It makes short, iterative development sessions cheaper and faster.

**What to do:**
- Check if you’re on Google AI Pro or Ultra and open Google AI Studio to claim your monthly credits.
- Allocate credits to experiments, model hosting, or short agent tests and watch billing in Studio.
- Move quick development work into Studio to keep costs predictable and speed up iterations.
