**TL;DR:** Gmail's "Help me write" AI drafting feature is rolling out to all Google Workspace users 18 and older over the next two weeks.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and entrepreneurs spend a surprising amount of time on email that wins or loses work. A native drafting assistant speeds writing, keeps tone consistent, and cuts the back-and-forth. Because it lives inside Gmail, you don’t have to juggle extra apps.

**What to do:**
- Use "Help me write" for three repeatable emails this week: cold outreach, proposals, and payment reminders.
- Tweak the AI drafts once, then save the best versions as templates to reuse.
- Optional: If you don’t see the feature, check with your Workspace admin about rollout timing.
