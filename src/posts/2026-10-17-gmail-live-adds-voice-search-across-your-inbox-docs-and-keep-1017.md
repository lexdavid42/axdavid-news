**TL;DR:** Google is rolling out Gmail Live to AI-plan subscribers, adding voice search across your inbox and companion features Keep Live and Docs Live.

**Why it matters:** You can pull messages, notes and documents with a voice query instead of digging through folders. That saves minutes per lookup and keeps your hands free during calls or when on the move. Check permissions — those Live features surface content across Google apps.

**What to do:**
- Enable Gmail Live if you’re on an eligible AI plan and try simple voice queries for clients, receipts, or meeting notes.
- Test results on a small set of sensitive messages and review the app’s privacy and access settings.
- Optional: prototype a voice-based workflow (search → copy/link → task or calendar entry) using your automation toolchain.
