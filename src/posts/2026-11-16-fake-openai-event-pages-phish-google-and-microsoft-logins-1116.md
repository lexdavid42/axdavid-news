**TL;DR:** Attackers are using fake OpenAI event pages that promise free ChatGPT Pro to trick employees into handing over Microsoft and Google credentials.

**Why it matters:** This is a targeted credential-phishing campaign aimed at tech workers and contractors who use Google or Microsoft logins for work. Stolen credentials can give attackers access to email, client files, billing portals, and AI service accounts. Small teams and solo freelancers are especially vulnerable because a single compromised account can expose client data and billing.

**What to do:**
- Don’t enter work credentials on event pages — verify the event URL independently.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication and use a password manager for all work accounts.
- Report suspicious invites to your IT team, email provider, or the service being impersonated.
