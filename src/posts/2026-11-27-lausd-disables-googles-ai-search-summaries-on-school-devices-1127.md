**TL;DR:** Los Angeles Unified School District has turned off Google’s AI-generated search summaries (AI Overviews) on school-issued devices while it reviews the feature.

**Why it matters:** Schools are testing limits on AI features that touch student data. That can force districts to consider alternatives to major tech vendors. For freelancers and entrepreneurs this opens demand for privacy-first search tools, edtech integrations, and outreach to districts reevaluating vendor choices.

**What to do:**
- Audit any education clients’ device settings and advise whether AI summaries are enabled or blocked.
- Prepare SEO and content strategies assuming some users will not see AI Overviews and will click through to links.
- Optional: Pitch privacy-focused search or learning tools to districts exploring Google alternatives.
