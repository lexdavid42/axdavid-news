**TL;DR:** New research finds Google AI Overviews appear in 85% of shopping searches, pushing AI-curated suggestions into most buyer journeys.

**Why it matters:** Shoppers increasingly rely on AI summaries instead of scrolling search results. That means product discovery and buying intent can be decided by Google’s AI, not your ad or listing position. Sellers and freelancers who control product content stand to win more visibility and conversions.

**What to do:**
- Audit product titles, descriptions and structured data for clarity and buyer intent.
- Add high-quality images and concise benefit-led copy so AI overviews pick your products.
- Test listings in Google Shopping and track referral changes this season.
