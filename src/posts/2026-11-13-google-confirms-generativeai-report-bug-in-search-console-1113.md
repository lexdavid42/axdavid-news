**TL;DR:** Google confirmed a data logging bug that reduced impressions in the Search Console Generative AI performance report for Nov 9–11.

**Why it matters:** That report feeds client updates, content testing, and SEO decisions. Metrics for those three days may be understated and could trigger wrong conclusions. If you bill or pivot by short-term swings, this could affect invoices and tests.

**What to do:**
- Check your Search Console Generative AI report and flag any data from Nov 9–11.
- Export the raw data, annotate reports, and avoid adjusting campaigns based on that window.
- Optional: Monitor Google’s status updates and re-run comparisons after the fix before making strategy changes.
