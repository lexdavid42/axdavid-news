**TL;DR:** Google is moving inline links higher in AI Mode and AI Overviews, making source URLs more visible — but Search Console still doesn’t report clicks from AI results.

**Why it matters:** This change can return referral traffic to websites and help publishers get credit for answers shown by Google’s AI. But without click data in Search Console, those gains may not appear in your normal analytics. That makes short-term attribution and ROI harder for content creators and freelancers.

**What to do:**
- Confirm your site is crawlable and uses clear canonical tags; make short, factual answer pages that AI can cite.
- Add UTM-tagged links and monitor server referrer logs to capture clicks Search Console misses.
- Optional: Test compact “answer” pages with clear source links to earn AI citations and direct traffic.
