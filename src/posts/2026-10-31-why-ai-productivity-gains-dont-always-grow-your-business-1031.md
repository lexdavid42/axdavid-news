**TL;DR:** Productivity wins from AI don't automatically translate into business growth.

**Why it matters:** Time saved is only valuable when it increases revenue, margins, or capacity to take on paying work. Many AI projects optimize tasks but miss the customers, conversions, or pricing that drive profit.

**What to do:**
- Tie every AI pilot to a clear revenue or capacity metric before you scale.
- Automate tasks that directly free billable hours or reduce acquisition cost.
- Optional: kill or rethink automations that save time but add no measurable value.
