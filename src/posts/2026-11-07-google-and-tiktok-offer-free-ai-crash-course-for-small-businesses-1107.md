**TL;DR:** Google and TikTok are running a free crash course that teaches small businesses how to use AI to build an online presence and find customers.

**Why it matters:** Many freelancers and solo owners don’t have time to test every AI tool. A short, guided seminar cuts the learning curve and gives practical tactics you can apply that day. Knowing which AI features actually drive customers saves time and ad spend.

**What to do:**
- Sign up for the free seminar and block an hour in your calendar.
- Bring three concrete goals (lead type, audience, or revenue target) and one piece of existing content to revise.
- After the session, run one quick AI test (an ad copy, a social post, or a booking flow) and measure results.
