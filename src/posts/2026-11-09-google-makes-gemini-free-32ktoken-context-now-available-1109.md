**TL;DR:** Google now offers Gemini for free; the free tier uses the Flashlight model with a 32,000‑token context window.

**Why it matters:** Free access to a model with a huge context window lets freelancers and founders feed long documents, draft proposals, and review client work in one pass. That cuts chunking, slows fewer interruptions, and can replace some paid tools for early drafts and research.

**What to do:**
- Sign into Gemini and run a real long-document test (proposal, contract, or client research) to see how the 32K context handles it.
- Compare outputs with your current LLM for accuracy, instruction-following, and editing speed on those same documents.
- If results match or exceed your workflow, use Gemini for draft work and reserve paid models only for final or high-risk tasks.
