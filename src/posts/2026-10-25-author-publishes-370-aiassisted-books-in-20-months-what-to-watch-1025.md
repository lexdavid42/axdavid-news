**TL;DR:** A South African author has published 370 AI-assisted books in 20 months and faced intense online backlash and threats.

**Why it matters:** This shows how fast AI can produce scalable content and create income streams for writers and publishers. It also highlights real reputational and safety risks when output is perceived as low-effort or deceptive. Freelancers must balance speed with quality and clear disclosure.

**What to do:**
- If you use AI to publish, label AI involvement clearly and set a quality review process. 
- Focus on niche value: edit, localize, or add unique expertise that AI can’t replicate. 
- Monitor reputation channels and prepare a rapid response plan for harassment or legal threats.
