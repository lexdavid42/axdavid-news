**TL;DR:** Google is expanding its AI-powered messaging in Google Business Profiles to more businesses, letting profiles reply to customers automatically.

**Why it matters:** Entrepreneurs and freelancers can turn profile views into fast leads without answering every message. Automated replies cut response time and free your schedule. You still must check responses for accuracy and guard customer data.

**What to do:**
- Open your Google Business Profile and enable AI messaging where available.
- Edit and train the canned replies so they reflect your services, hours, and pricing.
- Monitor conversations daily and set a clear handoff to a human when needed.
