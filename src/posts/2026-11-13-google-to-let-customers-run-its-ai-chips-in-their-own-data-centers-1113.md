**TL;DR:** Alphabet is offering customers the ability to run its AI accelerators inside their own data centers, turning its chips into hardware customers can deploy on‑prem.

**Why it matters:** This gives freelancers and small firms new ways to lower inference costs and keep sensitive data off public cloud stacks. It also creates opportunities for agencies and managed‑service providers to offer hosted AI with better latency and privacy. Expect new pricing and hosting models to emerge.

**What to do:**
- Audit which AI tasks need low latency, lower per‑call costs, or extra data privacy to justify on‑prem hardware.
- Talk to your infrastructure partners or Google sales about appliance options and migration costs.
- Recalculate total cost of ownership and consider packaging hosted on‑prem AI as a premium service.
