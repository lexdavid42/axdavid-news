**TL;DR:** Google has flipped on “Dive deeper” in AI Mode: AI answers now end with larger, thumbnail-rich link cards that are more prominent and clickable.

**Why it matters:** This directly affects how people discover your pages. Rich thumbnails and bigger cards can grab attention — for better or worse — and change which pages get referral traffic. Freelancers and small businesses that rely on organic clicks could see traffic shift fast.

**What to do:**
- Audit your high-value pages for Open Graph and structured metadata so thumbnails and descriptions render correctly.
- Add clear, compelling preview images and short meta descriptions to your landing and sales pages.
- Monitor referrals from Google and prioritize fixing pages that lose impressions or clicks.
