**TL;DR:** Most U.S. businesses that rank on Google’s first page don’t appear in AI assistant answers, leaving many brands invisible to shoppers.

**Why it matters:** AI assistants are becoming the first stop for buyers. They recommend brands by citation and concise answers — not by classic search rank. If assistants don’t cite you, potential customers will be steered to competitors even when you rank well.

**What to do:**
- Ask popular AI assistants your core customer queries and record which brands they cite.
- Create short, answer-ready content and clear citation signals (concise facts, FAQs, and structured references) your brand can be quoted from.
- Optional: Use an AI‑SEO audit or partner to benchmark citations and track improvement.
