**TL;DR:** Google pledged $15 million to support Indian MSMEs and expanded its free AI training for teachers, linking education, research and small-business support.

**Why it matters:** This increases practical AI resources aimed at small businesses—more demand for implementation and training services. If you sell AI setup, prompts, or workshops, this creates new customers and a repeatable service model. It also means more free training materials you can adapt and resell as workshops or templates.

**What to do:**
- Reach out to Indian clients or partners and offer AI audits and quick setup packages.
- Enroll in or review Google's free AI teacher training to build paid micro‑courses and templates.
- Package a low‑cost onboarding offer: 60‑minute setup, prompt library, and 30‑day support.
