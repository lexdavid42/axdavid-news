**TL;DR:** Google will let adult users in the US and select other countries upload AI-created Android apps through Google AI Studio without a paid developer account.

**Why it matters:** This removes a friction point for solo makers and small teams who build with AI. You can test app ideas and reach Android users faster. Faster launches mean quicker feedback and earlier revenue.

**What to do:**
- Sign up for Google AI Studio and explore its Android export path.
- Prototype a focused utility that automates a common freelance or small-business task.
- Optional: Prepare a simple privacy notice and a monetization plan (one-time fee, subscription, or in-app purchase).
