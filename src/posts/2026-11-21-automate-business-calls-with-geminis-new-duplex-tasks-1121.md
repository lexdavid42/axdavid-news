**TL;DR:** Google’s Gemini now runs Duplex “Tasks” to make and manage real business phone calls for you.

**Why it matters:** This lets freelancers and small businesses offload routine calls—appointments, bookings, order checks—so you save hours each week. Merchants can opt out, and Google explains how Tasks evolved from Duplex and differs from outbound Muse.

**What to do:**
- Test Gemini Tasks with low‑risk calls first (appointments, confirmations, vendor queries).
- Build simple call scripts and clear success/fail criteria; log outcomes for billing and compliance.
- Check merchant opt‑out and privacy settings before scaling automation.
