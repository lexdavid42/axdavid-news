**TL;DR:** Google is reportedly testing removing the clickable citations card from desktop AI Overviews, which would hide the direct source links behind AI answers.

**Why it matters:** Fewer visible source links means less organic search traffic for freelancers, writers, and small publishers. If AI answers become the destination, your referral clicks and leads could drop quickly.

**What to do:**
- Monitor your top pages for sudden drops in clicks and impressions this week.
- Make content easy to quote: short, clear answers and strong brand signals so AI can reference you.
- Build direct channels—email lists, social followers, or landing pages that convert without a search click.
