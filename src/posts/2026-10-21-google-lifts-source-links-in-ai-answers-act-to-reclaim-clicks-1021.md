**TL;DR:** Google is making source links more visible in AI-powered Search answers and surfacing sites users mark as “preferred sources,” increasing the chance publishers see clicks.

**Why it matters:** For freelancers and entrepreneurs who publish content, this can restore referral traffic that AI summaries had been eating. It changes how readers discover original pages — not just the AI summary. Reports show preferred-source clicks rose 7x in tests.

**What to do:**
- Add clear, factual lead paragraphs and obvious citations so AI can pick your page as a source.
- Prompt loyal readers to mark your site as a preferred source (email, account settings, or site banners).
- Monitor referral traffic and tweak titles, snippets, and structured data to improve click-throughs.
