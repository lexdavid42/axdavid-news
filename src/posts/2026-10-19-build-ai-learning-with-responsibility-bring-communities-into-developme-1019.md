**TL;DR:** DeepMind’s Lila Ibrahim says AI-powered learning must be built with responsibility and by involving the communities it will affect.

**Why it matters:** Entrepreneurs and freelancers building courses, tools, or training services will face demand for accountable, locally relevant AI products. Products that ignore bias or local context will struggle to scale or win trust. Positioning services around responsible design can become a direct revenue and differentiation advantage.

**What to do:**
- Audit your AI learning products for cultural fit and obvious biases.
- Run small paid pilots with target communities and iterate on feedback.
- Publish a short Responsible AI checklist for clients and use it in proposals.
