**TL;DR:** Sarvam launched an AI dictation API, Sarvam Dictate, that converts speech into near-finished text across English and 22 Indian languages and says it needs fewer edits than Google’s Gemini.

**Why it matters:** Faster, more accurate transcription cuts post-production time for interviews, client calls, captions and notes. Freelancers and small teams can turn raw audio into deliverables quicker. Developers can embed the API to automate captioning, meeting summaries, and multilingual content pipelines.

**What to do:**
- Run a quick A/B test: send 5–10 representative recordings to Sarvam Dictate and to your current tool and compare edit time. 
- If results hold, route routine transcription tasks through the API to free up time for higher-value work. 
- Optional: ask for pricing, latency, and language-specific accuracy details before full integration.
