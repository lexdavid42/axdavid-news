**TL;DR:** A new Chrome cheat sheet walks through built-in features like Gemini AI, Auto Browse, privacy controls, and enterprise tools so you can work faster and safer.

**Why it matters:** Chrome now bundles productivity and safety tools that can shorten research, speed workflows, and protect client data. Small teams and solo freelancers can get big-time gains without adding new apps. Skipping a quick settings review leaves time and data on the table.

**What to do:**
- Turn on Gemini and test Auto Browse with a real research task to judge speed and accuracy.
- Open Privacy & Security settings and lock down what you don’t want shared with AI features.
- Optional: Use Chrome profiles or Enterprise controls to separate client work and improve performance.
