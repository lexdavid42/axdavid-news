**TL;DR:** Google is shutting down Opal on Nov. 17, and your saved AI workflows and apps won’t migrate automatically.

**Why it matters:** If you use Opal for automations, scheduled tasks or client workflows, you risk losing access and billable processes. There’s no automatic transfer — you must export and rebuild or your business could face downtime and lost IP.

**What to do:**
- Export all Opal projects, workflow files, and linked assets now and store them offline.
- Download configs, API keys, and any connected data; then rotate keys and update credentials.
- Recreate critical automations in an alternative platform or script them locally; test immediately.
