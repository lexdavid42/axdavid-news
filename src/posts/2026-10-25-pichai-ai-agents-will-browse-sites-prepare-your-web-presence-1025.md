**TL;DR:** Google CEO Sundar Pichai says AI agents will reach websites by both browsing pages and by using machine-readable protocols; protocols scale better but will take longer to appear.

**Why it matters:** This affects how AI tools discover and reuse your content, and whether your site drives real clicks or just feeds answers. Browsing agents behave like human visitors; protocols give you control and a chance to monetize or limit access. Small businesses and freelancers must decide how to expose critical content.

**What to do:**
- Log and analyze automated traffic now; identify pages agents hit and optimize those pages with clear calls to action.
- Add machine-readable signals (sitemaps, structured data, or a simple API) to control what agents should index and how to contact you for commercial use.
- If heavy automated access hurts conversion, test access controls or paid API routes.
