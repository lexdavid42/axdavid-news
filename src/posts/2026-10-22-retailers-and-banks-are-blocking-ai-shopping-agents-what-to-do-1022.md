**TL;DR:** Major retailers and banks are blocking autonomous shopping agents, limiting their ability to sign in, place orders, or access accounts.

**Why it matters:** If you build or sell shopping automation, these blockers break core integrations and revenue paths. Courts, merchants and banks are shaping what agents can do — not the tech. You need practical fallbacks now, not slogans.

**What to do:**
- Audit every product that automates buying or account access; test logins, checkout flows and payment access immediately.
- Build partner-friendly paths: prioritize official APIs, browser-extension flows, or retailer-approved integrations over headless account automation.
- (Optional) Shift some offers to paid concierge services or consulting while platform access stays uncertain.
