**TL;DR:** Google rolled out Temporary Threads in AI Mode for Search — a quick, Incognito-style session that stops AI conversations from being saved to your account.

**Why it matters:** This gives freelancers and solo founders a fast way to run confidential research, test prompts, and draft client work without creating a persistent AI history. That lowers the risk of leaking sensitive project details or polluting your personalized model with one-off queries. It also makes experimenting with risky prompts safer.

**What to do:**
- Use Temporary Threads for sensitive research, pricing checks, and client drafts you don’t want stored.
- Treat Temporary Threads as disposable: copy anything you need to your own notes before closing the session.
- Compare results between Temporary Threads and regular AI Mode to see if personalization changes outcomes.
