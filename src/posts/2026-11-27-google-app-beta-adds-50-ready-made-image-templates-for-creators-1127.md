**TL;DR:** Google’s Android app beta now hides a gallery of over 50 ready-made visual prompts and templates behind the Create button to speed AI image generation.

**Why it matters:** This cuts time spent writing image prompts. Freelancers and entrepreneurs can produce consistent visuals faster without learning prompt engineering. It lowers the barrier to creating marketing assets, thumbnails, and social visuals.

**What to do:**
- Open the Google app beta (Create > Visual templates) and scan the gallery for styles you can reuse.
- Copy or adapt a template for your brand; tweak one or two words and run the image generator to test variants.
- Keep a personal library of 5–10 favorite templates for quick social, ad, and product imagery.
