**TL;DR:** Google will remove access to the higher‑tier Gemini Pro model from the $5 Google AI Plus plan on November 13; Flash and Flash‑Lite models remain available.

**Why it matters:** Many freelancers and solo entrepreneurs use Google AI Plus for quick drafting, research, and content tasks. Losing Pro model access can lower output quality or speed for some workflows. You may need to upgrade, change prompts, or switch providers to keep the same results.

**What to do:**
- Check which Gemini model your current workflows rely on and test Flash/Flash‑Lite now.
- Compare the $19.99 AI Pro tier cost vs. revenue from tasks that need Pro output; plan upgrades only if profitable.
- Optional: trial alternative LLMs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Claude) for the same tasks before committing.
