**TL;DR:** Google announced Agent Compute Engine, a managed service that runs AI agents inside isolated microVMs while keeping credentials outside the sandbox; a preview is due in the coming months.

**Why it matters:** This could sharply reduce the risk that an automated agent leaks API keys or account credentials. Freelancers and small teams that run automation or client-facing agents get a path to safer, auditable execution. The preview window gives you time to plan migrations and cost tests.

**What to do:**
- Sign up for the preview and plan a simple test of one agent workflow.
- Audit any agents that hold secrets and prepare to externalize keys before migration.
- Optional: Measure latency and pricing impact by prototyping critical automations in a microVM-like environment.
