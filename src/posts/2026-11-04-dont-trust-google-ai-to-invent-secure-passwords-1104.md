**TL;DR:** Google Search’s AI Overviews can suggest weak, predictable passwords — don’t use them to secure accounts.

**Why it matters:** AI-generated suggestions from search overviews are often simple, repeated, or guessable. Entrepreneurs and freelancers rely on many online accounts; one weak password can cost time, reputation, and clients.

**What to do:**
- Use a vetted password manager to generate and store unique, long passwords.
- Enable two-factor authentication on all business and client accounts.
- Audit critical logins now and rotate any passwords created by AI tools.
