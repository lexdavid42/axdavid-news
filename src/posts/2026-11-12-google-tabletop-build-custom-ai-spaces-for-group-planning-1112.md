**TL;DR:** Google is testing Tabletop, a Labs experiment that lets people create shared AI-powered spaces for planning, coordinating, and playing in real time.

**Why it matters:** Tabletop turns AI from a solo assistant into a shared workspace you can shape for specific group tasks. Entrepreneurs and freelancers can build lightweight, collaborative tools for client planning, booking, or product roadmaps without heavy engineering. Early experiments mean less competition and a chance to prototype new services fast.

**What to do:**
- Join the Tabletop waitlist and reserve a test space for a client workflow or team calendar.
- Prototype one tight use case (client onboarding checklist, event plan, or pitch prep) and run a live trial with real users.
- Optional: document the flow and package it as a paid offering or template for other clients.
