**TL;DR:** Google is rolling a collapsible AI chat sidebar into the desktop Search homepage so you can resume recent Gemini chats and start new AI threads directly from Search.

**Why it matters:** This turns Google’s homepage into a quick hub for AI work. Entrepreneurs and freelancers can pick up research, proposals, or client Q&A without hunting through tabs or app windows. It shortens the loop between search and follow-up tasks.

**What to do:**
- Try the sidebar for a week: resume one existing chat and start one new thread for client work.
- Use it as a persistent scratchpad: move summaries and to-dos from chats into your project tool.
- If privacy is a concern, review AI mode history and settings immediately.
