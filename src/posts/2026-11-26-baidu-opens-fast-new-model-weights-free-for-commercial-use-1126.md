**TL;DR:** Baidu released the weights of a new high‑speed reasoning model and made them available for commercial use.

**Why it matters:** Developers can run a capable model locally or on cheaper cloud instances, cutting API bills and latency. That makes it easier for freelancers and small teams to ship faster, cheaper AI features. It also gives you leverage when negotiating vendor costs.

**What to do:**
- Download the weights and run a quick benchmark on one real task you already sell.
- Compare inference cost and latency against your current API provider.
- If the numbers look good, plan a staged migration or a lower‑cost offering for clients.
