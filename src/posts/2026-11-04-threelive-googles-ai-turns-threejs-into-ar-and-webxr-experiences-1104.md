**TL;DR:** Google’s Three‑Live is an open‑source AI that converts Three.js code into interactive 3D models and ready-to-run AR/WebXR experiences for web and mobile.

**Why it matters:** This cuts hours from building and prototyping 3D web experiences. Freelancers and small studios can deliver AR demos and interactive models faster without deep WebXR plumbing. It lowers the barrier to add premium, billable 3D features to sites and pitches.

**What to do:**
- Try Three‑Live on a small client demo to test workflow and output quality.
- Replace a manual Three.js prototype with an exported Three‑Live build to speed delivery.
- Optional: Fork the repo or watch the docs to reuse generated templates in your services.
