**TL;DR:** Google opened a Connected Apps preview so developers can apply to bring apps into AI Mode and the Gemini app; its review rules ban misleading pitch language.

**Why it matters:** Being accepted puts your app directly inside AI-powered search and Gemini conversations—huge distribution without building a new front end. The policy note means your app listing and claims must be accurate or risk rejection.

**What to do:**
- Apply to the Connected Apps preview and reserve a slot for your highest-value integration.
- Rewrite app descriptions to be precise about capabilities and data use to meet Google's policy.
- Prepare short, tested sample prompts and a safe, predictable multi-turn flow for Gemini review.
