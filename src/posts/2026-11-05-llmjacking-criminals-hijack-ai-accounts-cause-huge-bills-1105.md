**TL;DR:** New research from Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence Group shows criminals are “LLMjacking” — hijacking enterprise AI access and running up fraudulent model charges that topped $100,000 in a single day.

**Why it matters:** This can drain your cloud credits and interrupt client work without obvious signs. Stolen model access can also expose prompts, data, and intellectual property your business relies on.

**What to do:**
- Rotate and revoke API keys immediately and store keys in a secrets manager.
- Set strict billing alerts, daily usage caps, and role-based access for AI services.
- Monitor model calls and anomalous patterns; automate alerts and block unknown IPs.
