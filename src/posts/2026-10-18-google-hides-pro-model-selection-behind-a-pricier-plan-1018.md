**TL;DR:** Google is removing manual Pro model selection from AI Plus on November 13; keeping that choice requires moving to a subscription about four times more expensive.

**Why it matters:** If you pick specific Pro models for client work, automations, or higher-quality outputs, those workflows can break or become much costlier. Freelancers and small teams who rely on model choice will need to decide whether to pay up, switch vendors, or rebuild pipelines.

**What to do:**
- Audit where your tools or services explicitly select Pro models and note which are essential.
- Test alternative providers or open models now and export prompts, settings, and test suites.
- If you must stay, negotiate enterprise pricing or build a cost model to see if the pricier tier is worth it.
