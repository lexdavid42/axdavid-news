**TL;DR:** AI-powered cyberattacks are surging and are already overwhelming major US defenders like Google Mandiant, CrowdStrike and Kroll.

**Why it matters:** Attacks that use generative AI create more convincing phishing and faster, automated exploit campaigns. Small businesses and freelancers are low-friction targets. If your systems or client data get hit, recovery will be costly and slow.

**What to do:**
- Patch systems, enable multi-factor authentication, and lock down remote access now.
- Treat unexpected links, invoices, and file requests as high-risk; use an AI-aware phishing filter or sandbox attachments.
- Backup critical data, rehearse an incident plan, and consider an endpoint solution with AI-detection.
