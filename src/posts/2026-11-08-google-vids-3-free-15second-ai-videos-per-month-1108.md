**TL;DR:** Google Vids now lets people with a Google account or Workspace create up to three free 15‑second AI videos per month.

**Why it matters:** Short social videos sell products and services faster than text. This gives freelancers and small teams a free, no-install way to test short ads, reels, and promos. The monthly cap means you must use slots strategically.

**What to do:**
- Make one 15‑second promo for your top offer and test it on Reels or TikTok.
- Store brand assets and clear prompts in a shared doc for quick re-runs.
- Optional: Schedule a monthly content slot plan so you don’t waste freebies.
