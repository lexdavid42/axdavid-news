**TL;DR:** Google rolled out new Gemini AI productivity tools aimed at small business owners to automate daily tasks, analyze finances, draft landing pages and connect apps.

**Why it matters:** These tools can cut hours from routine admin and give quick financial signals without hiring help. Faster landing page and app work lets freelancers and entrepreneurs test offers and move faster.

**What to do:**
- Pick one repetitive task (invoicing, replies, or reports) and run Gemini on it for a week to measure time saved.
- Run a short finance check: give Gemini a month of numbers and ask for three prioritized actions.
- Optional: generate a landing page draft with Gemini, publish a quick A/B test, and iterate.
