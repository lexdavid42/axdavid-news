**TL;DR:** Google expanded its eighth-generation TPU lineup with two new systems: the TPU 8tx for hardware‑encrypted confidential compute and the TPU 8i Max for high‑density context workloads, joining existing 8t and 8i models.

**Why it matters:** These chips make it easier to run agentic AI that needs long context and to process sensitive customer data in the cloud. That opens product opportunities for freelancers and small teams building conversational agents, document-heavy workflows, or regulated-data services.

**What to do:**
- Audit any AI features that handle client-sensitive data and mark candidates for confidential compute.
- Plan tests for long‑context models and measure latency and cost on equivalent cloud instances.
- Contact your cloud rep for early access, pricing, and migration guidance.
