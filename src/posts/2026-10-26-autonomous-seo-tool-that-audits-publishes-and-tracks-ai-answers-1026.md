For freelancers and small businesses, that could mean fewer lost clicks and less time fiddling with technical SEO. Treat the claim as a lead — verify it on your site before handing access.

**What to do:**
- Request a demo or trial and run Ryze's audit against a high-value page.

- Optional: track brand citations it reports and prioritize changes that recover referral traffic.
