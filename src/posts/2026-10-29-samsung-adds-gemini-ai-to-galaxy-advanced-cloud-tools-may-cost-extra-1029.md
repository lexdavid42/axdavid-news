**TL;DR:** Samsung is expanding Google Gemini on new Galaxy phones with single-prompt photo tools, cross-app automations, and deeper system controls — but some advanced cloud AI features may land behind a paid subscription.

**Why it matters:** These features can speed content creation and automate routine workflows on your phone. That saves time for busy freelancers and founders who work on mobile. A subscription requirement would add a recurring cost and could gate the fastest, cloud-powered tools.

**What to do:**
- Try the new on-device features on a test phone to see which cut hours from your workflow.
- Budget for a possible subscription if cloud-only tools become essential.
- Optional: map which tasks must stay local vs. cloud and keep backup workflows if costs rise.
