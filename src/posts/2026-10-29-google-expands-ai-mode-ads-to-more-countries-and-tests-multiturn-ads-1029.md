**TL;DR:** Google is rolling ads into AI Mode across new international markets and testing multiturn ads in the U.S.

**Why it matters:** This changes where search-driven customers see paid messages. Entrepreneurs and freelancers who sell services or run campaigns could lose organic visibility or gain new ad inventory depending on strategy.

**What to do:**
- Audit current search traffic and identify queries likely to show AI Overviews in the listed countries.
- Test small-budget AI-Mode ad creatives and measure conversions before reallocating spend.
- Track U.S. multiturn ad performance; pause or tweak campaigns that underperform in conversational placements.
