**TL;DR:** Google Cloud will run confidential AI workloads on AMD EPYC processors, adding hardware-enforced protection for TPU 8tx inference, fine-tuning and reinforcement learning.

**Why it matters:** This makes cloud training and inference safer for sensitive client data. Freelancers and small teams can move private models to managed infrastructure without exposing raw data. It also eases compliance for regulated work like health, legal, or finance.

**What to do:**
- Inventory which projects handle sensitive or regulated data and flag them for confidential compute.
- Test a small pilot: run inference or a fine-tune on a confidential TPU 8tx instance to check performance and cost.
- Update client contracts and data-flow diagrams to show hardware-backed protections where relevant.
