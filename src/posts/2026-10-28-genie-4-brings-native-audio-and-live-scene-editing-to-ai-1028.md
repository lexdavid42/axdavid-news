**TL;DR:** Google DeepMind revealed Genie 4, a world model that generates native audio and can edit live scenes; it’s available only to trusted testers for now, with a bigger Google AI Ultra on the roadmap.

**Why it matters:** This makes audio-first features and real-time visual edits practical for products and pitches. Freelancers can add voice interfaces, richer demos, and faster content revisions. Early access will set who wins the next wave of interactive services.

**What to do:**
- Audit your services for quick audio or live-edit use cases and pick two to prototype.
- Apply for tester programs or join waitlists and prepare short demo assets you can ship fast.
- Track Google AI Ultra announcements and plan which clients or products to upgrade first.
