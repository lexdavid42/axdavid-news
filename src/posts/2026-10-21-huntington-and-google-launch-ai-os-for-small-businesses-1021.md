**TL;DR:** Huntington National Bank, with help from Google, launched 1866 Studio — an AI-powered operating system aimed at small businesses (named for the bank’s founding year).

**Why it matters:** Banks bundling AI tools into a single platform changes where and how small businesses access automation, insights, and financial services. That can speed routine work for entrepreneurs and freelancers, but it also creates new platform dependencies and channel opportunities.

**What to do:**
- Ask your bank about 1866 Studio availability and what integrations it supports.
- List three repetitive tasks you’d hand off to an AI-backed bank OS (invoicing, cash flow checks, client follow-ups) and test alternatives.
- Optional: explore offering integrations or services the platform could resell.
