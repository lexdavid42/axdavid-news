**TL;DR:** The Reuters Institute finds newsrooms are shifting away from chasing SEO and toward building brand authority and direct audience relationships as AI search reduces traditional clicks.

**Why it matters:** AI-powered search often shows answers directly, cutting referral traffic from search engines. That hurts freelancers and small businesses that depend on organic search to find clients. Building a recognizable brand and owned distribution keeps you discoverable and trusted.

**What to do:**
- Stop optimizing only for keywords; publish original, useful work that proves your expertise.
- Invest in owned channels—email lists, newsletters, repeatable formats and direct outreach.
- Repurpose long content into short, clear summaries and FAQs that AI systems can surface as the go-to answer.
