**TL;DR:** Google Ads’ AI Max is making more automated choices in Search — you need to audit settings now to avoid wasted spend and poor leads.

**Why it matters:** Automation can save hours, but it can also widen who sees your ads and what copy runs. That can mean irrelevant clicks, higher costs, and low‑quality leads for solo founders and freelancers who live off tight ad ROI.

**What to do:**
- Audit recent search terms and add negative keywords to block irrelevant or low‑value traffic.
- Review and approve all AI‑generated headlines, descriptions, and asset combinations; pause anything off‑brand or misleading.
- Tighten conversion tracking and lead verification; limit automation on campaigns where lead quality matters most.
