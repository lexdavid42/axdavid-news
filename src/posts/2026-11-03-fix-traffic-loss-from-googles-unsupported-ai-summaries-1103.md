**TL;DR:** A new analysis finds about 1 in 9 claims in Google’s AI search summaries aren’t backed by their cited sources, and the system can bypass traditional search rankings.

**Why it matters:** That behavior sends answers to users without sending them to your site. Freelancers and small publishers who sell services or rely on ad revenue can see traffic and income slip when search answers concentrate on AI summaries.

**What to do:**
- Add clear, short factual summaries and visible citations at the top of key pages to increase the chance you’re the source AI uses.
- Convert passive readers into direct contacts: add email capture, push clients to book calls, and gate high-value resources.
- Optional: Monitor organic clicks and revenue for pages that previously drove traffic; prioritize fixes on the biggest losers.
