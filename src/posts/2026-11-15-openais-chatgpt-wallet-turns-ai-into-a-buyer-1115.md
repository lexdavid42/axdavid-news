**TL;DR:** OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, letting AI agents complete purchases for users using existing card networks.

**Why it matters:** This moves AI from recommending products to actually buying them. Freelancers and entrepreneurs can automate repeat purchases, subscription management, and new agent-driven commerce services. It also shifts where revenue and fees can be captured.

**What to do:**
- Create a test ChatGPT account and explore wallet settings and permissions.
- Rework product or service flows to accept agent-assisted checkout and handle receipts and refunds.
- (Optional) Draft clear consent and billing disclosures to avoid charge disputes.
