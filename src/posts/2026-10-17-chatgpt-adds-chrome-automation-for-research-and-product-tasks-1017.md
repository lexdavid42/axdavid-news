**TL;DR:** OpenAI is widening ChatGPT's role inside Chrome to automate research, product comparisons and other routine online tasks.

**Why it matters:** This gives freelancers and small teams another built-in browser assistant to cut time on online research and shopping decisions. It can replace repetitive copy-paste work and speed proposal or buying workflows. Expect faster task completion and less manual browsing.

**What to do:**
- Install or update the ChatGPT Chrome extension and grant the minimum permissions you need.
- Build 3–5 prompt templates for your common tasks (product comparisons, quick research, draft replies) and save them.
- Optional: run the same task with Gemini in Chrome to compare accuracy, speed and privacy settings.
