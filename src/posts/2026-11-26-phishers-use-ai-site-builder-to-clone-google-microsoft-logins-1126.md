**TL;DR:** Attackers used Hugging Face’s DeepSite AI website builder to create convincing fake Google and Microsoft sign-in pages and hosted them on the same platform where developers share AI demos.

**Why it matters:** This lowers the bar for phishing. Small teams and freelancers who rely on cloud accounts and shared demos are easy targets. Credentials stolen this way can break client work, billing, and access to sensitive files.

**What to do:**
- Turn on multi-factor authentication (use app or hardware keys) for all cloud and email accounts.
- Verify domains before entering credentials and avoid signing in on unfamiliar demo links.
- Run phishing drills and add URL checks to client onboarding and shared-demo policies.
