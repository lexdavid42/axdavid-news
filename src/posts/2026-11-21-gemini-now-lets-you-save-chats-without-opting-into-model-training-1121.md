**TL;DR:** Google’s Gemini app splits its activity controls so you can save chat transcripts without also consenting to have them used to train Google’s models.

**Why it matters:** This separates convenience from data sharing. Freelancers and small businesses who use Gemini for client work can keep records while reducing the risk their conversations are bundled into training datasets.

**What to do:**
- Open Gemini settings and separate “chat history” from “training” or data-sharing toggles.
- Export and securely store any client-sensitive conversations you must retain.
- Review your contracts and avoid pasting confidential client data into AI prompts unless you explicitly opt in.
