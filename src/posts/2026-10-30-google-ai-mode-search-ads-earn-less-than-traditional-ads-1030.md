**TL;DR:** Google’s SVP Philipp Schindler said ads shown inside AI Mode monetize at a lower rate than traditional search ads.

**Why it matters:** This changes the economics of search traffic many freelancers and entrepreneurs depend on. Fewer dollars per ad click or impression means lower ad revenue and possibly less paid inventory for direct-response campaigns.

**What to do:**
- Audit recent search traffic and ad revenue trends and compare pre-AI vs post-AI periods.
- Capture value earlier: push lead captures, email signups, and direct CTAs before users see AI summaries.
- Test and diversify: run experiments with ad creative, landing pages, and other channels (social, email) to offset lower search monetization.
