**TL;DR:** Semrush reports Google AI Overviews appear on 88% of tracked commercial keywords, while studies show AI-driven click-throughs remain very low.

**Why it matters:** This changes how search delivers customers — many queries now surface a single AI summary instead of multiple links. That can shrink organic clicks for product and service pages. Entrepreneurs and freelancers must treat AI answers as a new distribution channel, not just another SERP feature.

**What to do:**
- Make the first 40–60 words of key pages concise, answer-focused, and second-person ready for AI summarizers.
- Add clear schema (FAQ, Product, HowTo) and citeable facts so AI systems can source your site.
- Monitor which keywords show Overviews and shift paid, email and direct channels to protect conversions.
