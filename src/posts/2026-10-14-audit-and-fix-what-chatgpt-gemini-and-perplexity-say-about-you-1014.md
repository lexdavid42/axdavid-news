**TL;DR:** A new guide explains how AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) source information about people and shows how to audit and correct inaccuracies that shape first impressions.

**Why it matters:** Clients and partners now often get a quick profile from chatbots before they meet you. Errors or outdated info can cost trust and opportunities. Fixing what those systems repeat protects reputation and revenue.

**What to do:**
- Ask major chatbots what sources they used for information about you and collect links.
- Update and centralize accurate profiles on authoritative pages (your site, LinkedIn, industry bios).
- Publish a concise, AI-friendly bio or FAQ so models can find and cite the right facts.
