**TL;DR:** Google is partnering with ten U.S. states to expand free AI skills training and workforce development programs.

**Why it matters:** This creates low-cost upskilling for small businesses, freelancers, and contractors who need practical AI skills fast. Employers can tap a growing pool of trained workers without long hiring cycles. Faster access to training means you can automate tasks, add new services, or bid on higher-paying projects sooner.

**What to do:**
- Visit your state workforce or Google’s training page to find local program details and enrollment options.
- Enroll yourself or a key contractor in quick modules that teach practical AI tools you can apply this week.
- Optional: Partner with local training grads for short-term gigs to test new AI workflows.
