**TL;DR:** Google has opened the Gemini “Tasks” agent (formerly Spark) to free users in the US, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia and India.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and small business owners can now use an agent that automates routine work without an immediate subscription. That cuts time on email, scheduling and repetitive admin. It lowers the barrier to testing agent-driven workflows for billing, outreach and ops.

**What to do:**
- Open the Gemini app and look for the Tasks mode in the assistant options.
- Run one real, repetitive workflow (draft an outreach email or schedule a week of meetings) and measure time saved.
- Optional: review any confirmation or purchase prompts before letting Tasks complete sensitive actions.
