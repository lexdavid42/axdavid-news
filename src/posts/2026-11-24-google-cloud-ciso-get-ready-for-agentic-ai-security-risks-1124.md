**TL;DR:** Google Cloud’s CISO warns that agentic AI — systems that act autonomously — is changing cyber risk and how security teams operate.

**Why it matters:** Agentic AI can probe, exploit, and move faster than human teams. Small businesses and freelancers who delegate tasks to AI agents face new attack surfaces, stolen credentials, and automated fraud risks. Protecting access and telemetry is now as important as good backups.

**What to do:**
- Audit every AI agent, plugin, and API key you use; revoke unused keys.
- Apply least-privilege: give agents only the access they need and require human approval for dangerous actions.
- Turn on logging and alerting for agent behavior; rotate credentials and enable MFA everywhere.
