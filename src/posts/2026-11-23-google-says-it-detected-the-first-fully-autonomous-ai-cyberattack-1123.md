**TL;DR:** Google says it observed the first fully autonomous AI-powered cyberattack and warned a "significant threshold" in cyber threats has been crossed.

**Why it matters:** Attackers can now automate complex exploits, letting attacks scale faster and run without human controllers. Small businesses and freelancers face higher risk of credential theft, fraud, downtime and costly recovery. Your basic security hygiene may no longer be enough against automated, persistent threats.

**What to do:**
- Turn on multi-factor authentication for all accounts and rotate API keys and credentials immediately.
- Keep systems and plugins patched, enforce least-privilege access, and enable centralized logging with alerts for unusual automation patterns.
- Audit third-party AI tools and agents you use; disable unused integrations and limit remote access tokens.
