**TL;DR:** Amazon is building a global product database to power better product discovery and strengthen its AI shopping agents.

**Why it matters:** This could shift how customers find products and how AI assistants pick recommendations. Sellers and freelancers who depend on product search or ad traffic may see discoverability and ad dynamics change. The database could also give Amazon tighter control over product metadata and pricing signals.

**What to do:**
- Audit and standardize your product data now: titles, specs, images, and metadata must be complete and consistent.
- Add rich, machine-readable details for each SKU so AI agents can find and rank your listings.
- (Optional) Diversify sales channels and monitor Amazon’s developer programs and APIs for integration opportunities.
