**TL;DR:** Reports say OpenAI plans to buy AI chips from Google, potentially reducing its reliance on NVIDIA.

**Why it matters:** This could shift where large AI workloads run and loosen Nvidia’s grip on supply and pricing. Entrepreneurs and freelancers who depend on cloud GPUs could see new instance options or price movement. Vendor competition may force cloud providers to change billing and contracts.

**What to do:**
- Audit your current AI spend and note where you’re tied to specific hardware or clouds.
- Run a small benchmark on alternative cloud instances as soon as new options appear.
- Optional: add contract clauses that let you switch providers if pricing or availability changes.
