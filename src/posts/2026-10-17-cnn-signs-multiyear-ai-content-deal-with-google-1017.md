**TL;DR:** CNN announced a strategic multiyear partnership with Google that gives Google expanded content rights to experiment with generative AI features using CNN journalism.

**Why it matters:** This signals a new distribution and licensing path for high-quality editorial content. Freelance writers and independent publishers should watch how AI partnerships change referral traffic, licensing expectations, and revenue opportunities. The deal could shape what kinds of stories are selected for AI-powered summaries and who gets paid.

**What to do:**
- Review your content licensing and copyright terms; make sure you can negotiate reuse for AI products.
- Track referral and engagement sources now so you can spot shifts once AI features roll out.
- Pitch evergreen explainers and deep guides that AI agents would likely reuse.
