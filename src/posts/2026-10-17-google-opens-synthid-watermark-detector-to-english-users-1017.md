**TL;DR:** Google has opened English-language access to its SynthID Detector, a tool that scans images and videos for embedded AI watermarks.

**Why it matters:** This gives creators, reporters, and freelancers a quick way to check whether a file was AI-generated or marked as such. That can speed fact-checking, protect reputation, and reduce the time you spend chasing down suspicious media. It’s timely: platforms and elections are already a battleground for misleading visuals.

**What to do:**
- Test the detector on any questionable images or clips before publishing or sharing.
- Add a quick watermark-check step to your editorial or client workflow.
- If you publish originals, consider using or requesting provenance tools to prove authenticity.
