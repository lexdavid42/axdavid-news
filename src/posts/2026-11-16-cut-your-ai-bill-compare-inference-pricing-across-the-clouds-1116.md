**TL;DR:** A new comparison breaks down inference costs across AWS Bedrock, Azure OpenAI and Google Vertex AI, covering token rates, caching, batching and provisioned capacity.

**Why it matters:** Cloud inference costs can make or break a small AI product or freelance service. Misconfiguring tokens, skipping caching or choosing the wrong billing model can quietly erase profit. Knowing where costs hide lets you price services accurately and keep margins.

**What to do:**
- Run a short prototype on each provider to measure token and per-call costs for your typical prompts.
- Add caching and batching to reduce repeated inference and test provisioned vs on-demand pricing for steady workloads.
- Set cost alerts, model-size limits and factor API billing into client quotes before scaling.
