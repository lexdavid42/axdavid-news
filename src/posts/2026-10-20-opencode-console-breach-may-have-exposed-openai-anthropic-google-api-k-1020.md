**TL;DR:** OpenCode says a console breach may have exposed API keys for OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, affecting about 1.4% of users and prompting revocation notices.

**Why it matters:** If you run tools or services that use those keys, attackers could rack up bills, steal data, or break integrations. Freelancers and small businesses can face immediate downtime and unexpected charges if keys are abused.

**What to do:**
- Check your email and the OpenCode dashboard for key-revocation alerts now.
- Immediately rotate any exposed keys, revoke unused keys, and replace them in production.
- Audit recent usage and billing, restrict key scopes, add usage limits, and move secrets to a vault.
