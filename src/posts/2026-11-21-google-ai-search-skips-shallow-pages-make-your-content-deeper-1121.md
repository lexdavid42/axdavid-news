**TL;DR:** Google says AI-powered search prefers deeper, higher-quality pages and shows less interest in shallow content.

**Why it matters:** AI summaries and source-following reduce the value of thin, low-effort pages for discovery and traffic. Freelancers and small publishers who rely on quick, short pages will see less organic attention unless they adapt.

**What to do:**
- Combine answers with original analysis, case studies, or tools that only you can provide.
- Turn thin pages into useful assets: consolidate scattered posts into long how‑tos or downloadable templates.
- Audit your top pages and add depth, unique data, or a creator signal (author bio, consistent voice).
