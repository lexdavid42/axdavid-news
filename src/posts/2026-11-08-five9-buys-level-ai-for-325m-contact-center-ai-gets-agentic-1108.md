**TL;DR:** Five9 bought Level AI for $325 million to fold agentic AI, agent assist and conversation intelligence into its Genius CX contact-center platform.

**Why it matters:** This accelerates practical automation for support teams. Entrepreneurs and freelancers who manage customer service can cut handle times, add AI-driven coaching, or offer new managed-AI support services.

**What to do:**
- Audit your top 3 recurring support tasks to spot where agent assist or automated agents could remove hours.
- Run a short pilot with Five9 (or a comparable vendor) to test agent assist and conversation-intel features on real calls.
- Optional: retrain or reposition human agents as AI supervisors and sell AI-enhanced support packages.
