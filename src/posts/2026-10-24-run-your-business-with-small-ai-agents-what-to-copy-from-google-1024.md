**TL;DR:** Google CEO Sundar Pichai uses small AI agents to summarize internal discussions and monitor compute usage so he can stay on top without reading every update.

**Why it matters:** You can borrow the same pattern to save hours each week. Agents can condense meetings, spot budget leaks, and flag action items so you focus on decisions, not inboxes. This scales whether you’re solo or run a small team.

**What to do:**
- Build a meeting-summary agent that turns notes and transcripts into 3-bullet briefs and clear next steps.
- Create a cloud-cost agent that watches usage, sends alerts, and suggests shutdowns for idle resources.
- Optional: add an inbox-priority agent that highlights decisions and deadlines.
