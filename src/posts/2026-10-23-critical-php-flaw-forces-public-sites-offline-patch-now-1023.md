**TL;DR:** Researchers using AI tools disclosed a decades-old PHP vulnerability that forced government and public websites offline worldwide.

**Why it matters:** Many small businesses and freelancers run sites on PHP-powered platforms like WordPress. Unpatched servers can be taken offline or breached, costing hours of work and client trust. This is an urgent operational and security risk you can fix today.

**What to do:**
- Check your host and CMS alerts and apply any PHP or plugin updates immediately.
- If you can’t patch quickly, put affected sites into maintenance mode and inform clients.
- Run a quick security audit or hire a freelancer to scan for signs of compromise.
