**TL;DR:** Plaid now provides read-only bank connectivity to Instinct, letting U.S. users have personalized financial conversations with the AI.

**Why it matters:** Freelancers and small business owners can get faster, contextual answers about cash flow, balances, and transactions without manual uploads. That saves time on bookkeeping, invoicing follow-ups, and quick financial checks.

**What to do:**
- Connect your business accounts to Instinct using Plaid’s read-only option to enable instant, personalized finance Q&A.
- Ask Instinct for quick summaries: recent cash flow, unpaid invoices, or where you stand for upcoming bills.
- Audit and revoke access regularly if you change banks or tools.
