**TL;DR:** Visual Capitalist reports ChatGPT holds 56.4% of web visits to AI chatbots, while Gemini and Claude have captured noticeably more traffic this year.

**Why it matters:** Choose where you invest time and marketing based on real user reach. Rising share for Gemini and Claude means early-mover opportunities for freelancers and entrepreneurs who adapt fast.

**What to do:**
- Audit where your customers already interact with chatbots and prioritize integrations on the top platforms.
- Create and test short, shareable demos and prompts for Gemini and Claude now to gain visibility.
- Track monthly traffic and conversion by chatbot platform and shift paid spend or outreach to the best performers.
