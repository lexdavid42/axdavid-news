**TL;DR:** Google’s AI answers and ChatGPT favor brands that show up in credible media coverage, useful content, real reviews and solid technical SEO.

**Why it matters:** AI summaries can surface your business without a click, so visibility there equals discovery and trust. That can win customers faster than chasing organic click-throughs or expensive ads.

**What to do:**
- Pitch and earn placements in reputable outlets and trade sites.
- Publish short, answer-first pages (FAQs, how-tos) that directly solve customer questions.
- Encourage authentic reviews and make sure your site is crawlable and follows basic technical SEO best practices.
