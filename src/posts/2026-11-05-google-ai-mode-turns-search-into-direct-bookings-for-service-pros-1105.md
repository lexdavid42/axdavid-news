**TL;DR:** Google’s AI Mode now adds booking and quote links directly in home‑services search results, letting customers move from query to appointment pages.

**Why it matters:** This creates a new, high‑intent discovery channel for contractors, cleaners, landscapers and other service pros. Integrations from platforms like Jobber, Housecall Pro and Angi let requests flow into existing workflows. Partners and businesses are still sorting out lead fees and how to claim those bookings.

**What to do:**
- Claim and verify your business profile where customers search so AI Mode can surface your booking links.
- Check integrations (Jobber, Housecall Pro, Angi) and connect your scheduling software to accept requests automatically.
- Audit any partner lead fees and set clear pricing and booking rules before you accept AI‑generated leads.
