**TL;DR:** Google Ads is testing an Asset activity page that delivers weekly AI insight cards with creative suggestions, including headline ideas to test.

**Why it matters:** These cards give busy entrepreneurs and freelance marketers fast, ready-to-test creative options. They can cut the time you spend brainstorming headlines and jumpstart A/B tests. Freelancers can use the suggestions as deliverables or quick proof-of-work for clients.

**What to do:**
- Check your Google Ads account for a new Asset activity tab and enable the feature if available.
- Treat the AI headline ideas as test variants—set up quick A/B experiments and keep the winners.
- Optional: collect recurring AI suggestions into a swipe file to speed future campaigns.
