**TL;DR:** Google is rolling out a Gemini setting that lets you keep chat history while separately opting out of using your conversations to train its AI models.

**Why it matters:** This gives freelancers and entrepreneurs a clear privacy option when using Gemini for client work or sensitive planning. You can preserve conversation context without feeding those chats into model training. That lowers the risk of proprietary or client data being reused.

**What to do:**
- Open the Gemini app and review the new History & Training privacy controls.
- If you handle client or confidential data, disable model training while keeping history only if you need it.
- Optional: export critical chats and store them offline before clearing history.
