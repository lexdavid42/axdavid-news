**TL;DR:** SearchEngineJournal flags rank-data glitches, differences between PageSpeed lab and field scores, and a new shared AI-training opt-out that can affect traffic and indexing.

**Why it matters:** These issues can hide drops in organic clicks and slow conversions. Lab scores that don’t match real users lead to wrong fixes. The AI-training opt-out can change how your content is used by models and may affect visibility.

**What to do:**
- Check your rank reports for one-word results and redirect URLs; validate with live searches.
- Compare PageSpeed lab results to field (Real User) data before pushing performance changes.
- Review and set the AI training opt-out if you don’t want site content reused by large models.
