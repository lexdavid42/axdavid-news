**TL;DR:** YouTube Music launched an AI label that marks tracks made mainly with generative AI, and the company explains how the badge works, which tracks qualify, and why there’s no filtering yet.

**Why it matters:** This matters for musicians and anyone selling or licensing music. Platforms are moving toward explicit disclosure of AI-created work. That changes how listeners discover tracks and how you position your services or products.

**What to do:**
- Audit your catalog for AI-generated elements and note which tracks may need the label.
- Update metadata and artist pages to explain how you used AI and why the result adds value.
- Consider offering human-mixed or alternate versions if buyers or platforms prefer non-AI variants.
