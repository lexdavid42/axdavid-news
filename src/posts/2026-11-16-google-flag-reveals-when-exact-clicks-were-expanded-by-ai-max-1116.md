**TL;DR:** Google added a value — “a” — to the search_term_matchtype parameter to mark clicks that came from AI Max expansions, even on exact keywords; Google’s documentation is inconsistent on how it works.

**Why it matters:** This lets advertisers and freelancers spot which clicks came from AI-driven query expansions. That matters for attribution, bid decisions, and keyword performance reports. If you miss it, your “exact” keyword reports can be misleading.

**What to do:**
- Inspect your click logs and reporting for search_term_matchtype = a to separate AI Max traffic.
- Update dashboards and conversion models to treat those clicks differently for bidding and ROI calculations.
- Optional: Add negative keywords or refined match rules if AI expansions bring low-value traffic.
