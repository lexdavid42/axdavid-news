**TL;DR:** Google has moved AI Overviews for many brand-related searches into the right-hand column of the results page, downplaying their position in the main result stream.

**Why it matters:** This change shifts where users look on brand queries, reducing organic visibility and likely cutting click-throughs for firms and creators. Freelancers who depend on branded search traffic must expect lower referral volumes and faster attention decay from SERPs.

**What to do:**
- Audit your high-value brand and product keywords to see where AI Overviews now appear.
- Optimize for alternate SERP features (People Also Ask, featured snippets) and test paid placements for key brand queries.
- Build direct channels (email lists, repeat customers, social) to reduce reliance on unpredictable organic clicks.
