**TL;DR:** Perplexity has open-sourced SPACE, the Apache‑2.0 sandbox that powers its in‑house agent, and published a public repo and documentation.

**Why it matters:** This gives freelancers and small teams the actual runtime used to build a modern agent. You can inspect, run, and modify agent workflows instead of re-creating an agent stack from scratch. That cuts prototyping time and lowers the cost of shipping paid automation services.

**What to do:**
- Clone the SPACE repo and follow the hosted docs to run the sandbox locally and study agent behavior.
- Prototype a small, billable automation (lead research, email triage, content briefs) and iterate on prompts and connectors.
- Optional: package your vetted agent as a paid template or managed service—review the Apache 2.0 license and hosting costs first.
