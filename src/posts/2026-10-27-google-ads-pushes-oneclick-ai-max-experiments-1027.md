**TL;DR:** Google Ads may now surface a one-click recommendation to run AI Max experiments from the Recommendations page for eligible accounts.

**Why it matters:** This makes it far easier to test Google’s automated campaign changes without manual setup. Small businesses and freelancers can discover performance gains—or losses—fast. But one-click automation can also change spend and attribution quickly, so treat results as tests, not wins.

**What to do:**
- Check your Google Ads Recommendations page for a 1-click AI Max experiment suggestion and confirm eligibility.
- Run any experiment on a low-risk campaign with a capped budget and clear success metrics (CPA, ROAS, conversions).
- Optional: Pause or rollback immediately if cost or conversion quality drops.
