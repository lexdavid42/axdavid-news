**TL;DR:** Engadget shares practical prompting tips to get clearer, faster, and more useful responses from Google Gemini.

**Why it matters:** Gemini is a capable, widely available assistant for writing, coding, and research. Better prompts cut back-and-forth and speed delivery. That means more billable work, faster launches, and fewer edits.

**What to do:**
- Start with role + goal: tell Gemini who it should be and the specific outcome you want.
- Set constraints and examples: give length limits, tone, or a sample output to match.
- Iterate with short follow-ups: refine outputs in quick, focused requests rather than rewrites.
