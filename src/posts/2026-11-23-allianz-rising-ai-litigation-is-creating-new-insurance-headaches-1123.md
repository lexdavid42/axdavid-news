**TL;DR:** An Allianz commercial report warns that growing AI-related lawsuits and hidden exposures are driving a surge in corporate insurance claims.

**Why it matters:** More litigation means higher legal and insurance costs for businesses that use AI. Freelancers and small firms can face unexpected liability from tools or models they rely on. Ignoring contract and insurance gaps risks a surprise claim that can eat profits.

**What to do:**
- Inventory where you use AI and log any third‑party models, data sources, or automated decisions.
- Talk to your insurer or broker now about AI exclusions, coverage limits, and incident reporting requirements.
- Update client contracts and warranties to limit liability and require clear AI ownership and testing responsibilities.
