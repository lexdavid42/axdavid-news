**TL;DR:** OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic support a new shared robots signal that lets site owners stop AI crawlers from using their content to train models.

**Why it matters:** This gives creators and publishers an industry-backed, machine-readable way to protect original content. Freelancers and small publishers can control whether their work feeds AI models or is kept private. The change could shift how models source web text and how you monetize or license content.

**What to do:**
- Add the new robots signal to your site to explicitly block or allow AI training.
- Revise your content licensing and pricing if you plan to charge for model use.
- Monitor crawler logs and contact providers if you see ignored signals.
