**TL;DR:** Google Ads now offers a tool that clones English (US) Search campaigns into 10 new languages — and it copies budgets with no automatic currency conversion.

**Why it matters:** You can scale ads into new markets in minutes. But copied budgets and bids can cause overspend or mispriced bids in other currencies. Marketers and freelancers must check settings, creatives and tracking before hitting deploy.

**What to do:**
- Pause auto-copied campaigns and set conservative daily budget caps immediately.
- Convert and adjust bids for local currency and CPC expectations, and localize ad text and landing pages.
- Test one market at a time, monitor ROAS, and enable location-specific reporting.
