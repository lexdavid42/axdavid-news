**TL;DR:** Google Search Console now reports AI Search impressions through a Generative AI Performance Report, while AI-driven clicks still flow into standard web traffic metrics.

**Why it matters:** Your content can be seen by AI-driven answers without generating a tracked click. That makes traffic and ROI look smaller than the content actually produces. Freelancers and entrepreneurs must rework measurement and conversion tactics to capture value from AI visibility.

**What to do:**
- Check the Generative AI Performance Report weekly to spot which pages get AI impressions.
- Link impressions to conversions with UTM tags, server-side event tracking, or landing-page funnels.
- Optional: add clear, usable CTAs and lead magnets inside answers or schema so AI responses link back to measurable actions.
