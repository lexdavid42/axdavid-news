**TL;DR:** Google is prepping a live conversational AI "Chief of Staff" for Antigravity, with voice mode and a dashboard for extensions, approvals and background tasks as early as next week.

**Why it matters:** This turns an AI assistant from a text helper into a real-time, hands-off collaborator. Entrepreneurs and freelancers can offload routine approvals, scheduling and multi-step workflows. If it works, you could reclaim admin hours and scale services without hiring.

**What to do:**
- Line up one repetitive workflow (invoicing, outreach, approvals) to test with an agent.
- Prepare short prompts, clear approval rules and trusted extensions you’ll allow to run.
- Pilot voice-based checks for quick decisions before full rollout.
