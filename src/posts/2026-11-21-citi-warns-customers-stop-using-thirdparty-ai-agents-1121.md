**TL;DR:** Citi updated its user agreement and now warns customers against using third‑party AI agents with its online and mobile banking.

**Why it matters:** Using an external AI agent to log in, read balances, or initiate payments could violate your bank agreement and expose you to fraud or denied service. Freelancers and entrepreneurs who let automation handle invoicing, bill pay, or bookkeeping need to rethink which tools access live accounts.

**What to do:**
- Stop connecting third‑party AI agents to Citi online or mobile logins until you confirm compatibility.
- Switch to bank‑approved APIs, secure integrations, or manual approval steps for payments and transfers.
- Optional: tighten MFA, monitor transactions closely, and keep a clean audit trail for clients and taxes.
