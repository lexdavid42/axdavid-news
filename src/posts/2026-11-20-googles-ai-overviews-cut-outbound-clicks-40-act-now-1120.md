**TL;DR:** A randomized experiment found Google’s AI Overviews cut outbound link clicks by 40%, and AI summaries now show in about 61% of search results.

**Why it matters:** Less click-through traffic means fewer visitors to your site, fewer ad or affiliate conversions, and weaker organic leads. If your revenue depends on search referrals, this is a direct threat to cash flow.

**What to do:**
- Audit high-impression pages in Search Console and add concise answer boxes with a clear call-to-action above the fold.
- Build or double down on owned channels: email lists, push, and direct URLs so you control the audience.
- Test short paid campaigns and schema/structured data to reclaim visibility and appear as an authoritative source.
