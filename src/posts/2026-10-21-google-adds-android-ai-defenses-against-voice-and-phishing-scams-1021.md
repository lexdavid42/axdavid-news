**TL;DR:** Google added new Android protections to block scams that use spoofed calls, phishing links, fake alerts, and AI-generated voices.

**Why it matters:** Scammers are using AI to imitate voices and trick people into handing over money or credentials. Those losses hit freelancers and small businesses fast. Built-in defenses reduce risk and save time cleaning up fraud.

**What to do:**
- Update Android and Google apps now to get the new protections.
- Review and enable anti-scam settings, call screening, and link protections in your phone’s security menu.
- Turn on MFA for accounts and teach team members to verify unexpected calls or alerts.
