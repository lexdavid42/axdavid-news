**TL;DR:** The FBI and Google warn open-source AI tools are letting attackers automate and accelerate cyberattacks, letting breaches unfold in hours instead of days.

**Why it matters:** Automated attacks can drain your cash, lock customers out, or destroy client trust overnight. Small teams and freelancers are easy targets because attackers can run toolchains without human hackers. Defend fast or recover slowly—and expensively.

**What to do:**
- Patch exposed services now and prioritize critical CVEs within 24–72 hours.
- Rotate API keys and admin passwords; revoke unused credentials immediately.
- Turn on multi-factor authentication, enable detailed logging/IDS, and run an incident scan; isolate any suspicious hosts.
