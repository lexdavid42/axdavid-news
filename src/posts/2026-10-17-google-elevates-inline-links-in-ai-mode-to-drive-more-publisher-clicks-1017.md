**TL;DR:** Google is moving inline links, preferred-source carousels, and thumbnails higher in AI Mode and AI Overviews to make original sites more visible inside AI answers.

**Why it matters:** More prominent links mean a better chance of getting referral clicks from AI-generated responses. If you depend on website traffic, this narrows the gap between AI answers and direct visits. Expect Google to favor clear, verifiable sources.

**What to do:**
- Check AI Mode and AI Overview results for your top keywords and note which pages appear as links.
- Add concise, citation-ready lead paragraphs and schema (article, author, publishDate) so AI can surface your page.
- Track referral changes with analytics and adapt headlines or summaries to improve click appeal.
