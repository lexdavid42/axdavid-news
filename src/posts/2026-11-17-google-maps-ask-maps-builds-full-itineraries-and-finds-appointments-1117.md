**TL;DR:** Google Maps rolled out Ask Maps, a Gemini-powered feature that builds custom trip itineraries and adds appointment search with real-time traffic, parking and weather details.

**Why it matters:** This saves hours of trip prep for entrepreneurs and freelancers who travel for meetings or client work. It combines route planning, timing, and local logistics in one place so you can book smarter and move faster. Real-time parking and traffic checks cut no-shows and late arrivals.

**What to do:**
- Try Ask Maps for your next client visit or site call; ask it to plan the day and show travel windows.
- Use the appointment search to find available slots near your route and compare travel time before you book.
- Optional: Export or screenshot the itinerary and add time buffers for client calls and parking.
