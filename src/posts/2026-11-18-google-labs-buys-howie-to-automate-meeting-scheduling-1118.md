**TL;DR:** Google Labs has acquired Howie, an AI assistant that schedules meetings over email; Howie’s team will join Google and keep serving existing customers.

**Why it matters:** Busy freelancers and small-business owners spend hours on back-and-forth scheduling. A Google-owned scheduling agent could fold that work into Google's agent and calendar tools, freeing time and reducing admin friction. Expect faster, more polished meeting booking inside the Google ecosystem.

**What to do:**
- If you use Howie now, keep it active and note your integration settings so nothing breaks.
- Trial similar AI schedulers (or Howie) for client onboarding to measure time saved this month.
- Watch for Google rollout or Workspace features and plan to switch calendar workflows if they add deeper automation.
