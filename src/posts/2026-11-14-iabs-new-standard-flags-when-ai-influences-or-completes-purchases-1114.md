**TL;DR:** The Interactive Advertising Bureau released a framework to label and track when AI tools influence or complete purchases.

**Why it matters:** This gives advertisers, agencies and freelancers a shared way to measure AI-driven conversions. That helps fix attribution, protects commissions, and reduces disputes with clients. It also creates a new service opportunity: AI-attribution audits and reporting.

**What to do:**
- Audit your funnels now to map where AI (chat, recommendation, auto-checkout) touches the buyer journey.
- Add the IAB signals to your tracking plan or ask analytics vendors to support them.
- Update client contracts and commission rules to specify AI-influenced conversions.
