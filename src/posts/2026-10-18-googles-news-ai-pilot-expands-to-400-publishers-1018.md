**TL;DR:** Google expanded its paid News AI pilot to more than 400 publications, paying outlets for enhanced content used in its AI-powered features.

**Why it matters:** Publishers and independent writers now have a new path to get paid beyond subscriptions and ad revenue. If you produce written content, your distribution and referral traffic can shift as AI summaries rely on licensed material. This changes how content earns and where readers find it.

**What to do:**
- Audit what rights you own and what you can license for AI use.
- Package flagship stories or briefings as licensing-ready assets for direct deals.
- Monitor referral traffic and test small licensing offers to diversify income.
