**TL;DR:** AI-generated shopping ads are driving a rise in online scams before Black Friday, with buyers reporting missing orders, misleading promotions and payment compromises.

**Why it matters:** Scammers using AI ads can steal customers, refund money and ruin reputations for small sellers and freelancers. If you run ads, list products, or manage client campaigns, fake listings can hijack traffic and payments fast. This is a short-term spike with long-term trust costs.

**What to do:**
- Audit your paid ads and merchant listings daily; flag duplicates and fake creatives immediately.
- Move payments to secure, trackable gateways and require verification for high-value orders.
- (Optional) Add clear refund and dispute steps on product pages and keep order-tracking screenshots for disputes.
